TOUR DE ROTARY DSM
GOOGLE STITCH MASTER UI PROMPT PACK
2026 EDITION

PURPOSE
This file is the complete page-by-page prompt system for mocking the Tour de Rotary DSM digital platform in Google Stitch. Generate the product as a connected premium event platform, not as a collection of disconnected screens.

EVENT CONTEXT
Tour de Rotary DSM is a flagship Dar es Salaam charity cyclathon and multi-activity fundraising event supporting cancer care for children and women.
Event date: 1 November 2026.
Production platform handoff target: 15 September 2026.

CORE PRODUCT IDEA
One digital home for discovery, registration, secure checkout, event participation, merchandise, training preparation, social sharing, digital collectibles, volunteers, sponsors, partners and HQ operations.
The interface should feel reusable every year and phase-aware across PRE-EVENT, EVENT DAY and POST-EVENT.

DESIGN NORTH STAR
Think:
- Tour de France performance culture
- Dar es Salaam beach energy
- AAA sports game presentation quality
- Premium charity campaign credibility
- Modern African event identity

Do NOT make it look like a gaming dashboard, crypto website, fintech app, generic NGO site or generic ecommerce template.
Do NOT overuse gradients, glassmorphism, giant rounded cards, excessive shadows, neon colors or dark mode.
Do NOT make every section visually loud.
Keep hierarchy disciplined and editorial.

BRAND SYSTEM
Primary colors:
- Royal Navy
- Gold
- Cranberry Pink
- White
- Black

Background rule:
The core website background is WHITE. Use navy, gold and cranberry as accents, sections, borders, typography, badges, CTAs and graphic moments. Use photography and controlled dark panels for selected hero or immersive moments only.

COLOR BEHAVIOUR
Royal Navy: major headings, navigation, dark panels, key controls, route diagrams, serious operational surfaces.
Gold: premium action, achievement, sponsor tier, highlights, milestones, selected buttons, collectible accents.
Cranberry Pink: charity/cause emphasis, social moments, alerts, selected badges, feminine/health campaign accents.
White: dominant canvas, breathing room, readability.
Black: typography and high contrast details, used sparingly.

TYPOGRAPHY
Bold modern sans-serif with athletic editorial character.
Large display typography on marketing pages.
Compact clear UI typography for dashboards.
Use uppercase sparingly for labels and overlines.
Never sacrifice readability for style.

IMAGERY
Dar es Salaam should be visible throughout the visual language:
- Indian Ocean coastline
- palm-lined roads
- city streets
- sunrise or golden-hour riding
- beach-side community moments
- Tanzanian people participating naturally
- urban architecture and roads
- cycling, running, walking, Zumba and yoga

Do not stereotype Tanzania with safari imagery unless an event sponsor or story specifically requires it.
Use real-feeling local urban imagery.

GRAPHIC LANGUAGE
Use subtle:
- cycling route lines
- elevation/profile lines
- ribbon curves inspired by the cancer ribbon
- speed lines
- medal geometry
- road markings
- subtle topographic patterns
- coastline contours

Motion language:
- scroll-linked counters
- subtle parallax on major campaign imagery
- animated route line drawing
- progress rings
- smooth number transitions
- micro-interactions on badges, ticket cards and checkout
- celebratory but restrained confirmation states

ACCESSIBILITY
High contrast.
Large tap targets.
Clear focus states.
Avoid color-only status indicators.
Use labels with icons.
Design for mobile first, then desktop expansion.

GLOBAL INTERACTION PRINCIPLES
Every page should feel connected.
Every object that can be edited should have a clear path to the relevant action.
Use realistic TSh pricing, never dollar symbols in the UI.
All payment and order states should be visually clear.
All dashboards should prioritize what needs attention now.
Design empty states, loading states, success states and error states.

MASTER GENERATION RULE
============================================================
For every prompt below, create a polished production-quality page or screen in the Tour de Rotary DSM design system.
Use the assets supplied by the project owner where indicated. Do not invent alternate logos or redesign the supplied brand mark.
Keep the visual system consistent across the full product.
Prefer white canvas with strategic navy, gold and cranberry accents.
Include responsive behavior for desktop and mobile.
Use realistic Tanzanian TSh prices and event data.
Show interaction affordances clearly.
The screens should feel buildable in a real web application.
