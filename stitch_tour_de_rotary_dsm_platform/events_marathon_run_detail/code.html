The Ultimate Coastal Challenge

Push your limits on the stunning sands of Dar es Salaam. This isn't just a run; it's a test of endurance against the elements. Starting at dawn, experience the shift from cool morning breezes to the intense coastal sun as you navigate the challenging 21KM stretch.

Designed for seasoned athletes and determined challengers, the route offers varying terrain—from hard-packed wet sand to soft dunes. Expect a grueling but deeply rewarding journey, framed by the Indian Ocean.
