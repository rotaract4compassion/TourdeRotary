Registration & Prep

What is the deadline for early-bird registration? expand_more
Early-bird registration closes exactly 60 days prior to event day. We highly recommend securing your spot early as grid positions for the starting wave are determined by registration date.
Are e-bikes permitted on the route? expand_more

Yes, Class 1 and Class 3 e-bikes are permitted in the non-competitive casual waves. However, e-bikes are strictly prohibited in the Elite competitive wave to maintain fair timing standards.

Please ensure your battery is fully charged, as there are no charging stations along the coastal route.

Can I change my wave assignment later? expand_more
Wave changes are permitted up to 14 days before the event, subject to capacity. You can manage your wave assignment directly through your Rider Dashboard.
